# Cold drinks · the moving-drink footage

## What makes the reference feel alive

Measured on the reference reel frame by frame:

- **It is real footage.** Each cube moves on its own. It turns a little, sinks or rises, and its highlights and refraction change as it turns. The milk or syrup folds between the cubes, small bubbles rise and catch the light, and near and far cubes move at different speeds.
- **It is slow.** Local motion is about 8 px/s at 1080 wide. It reads as real because it happens in depth, not because it is fast.
- **The camera is locked.** There is no push, pan or zoom on the drink shots.
- **It cuts hard.** There is no dissolve, about every 1.5s on the beat. Our shots are 1.55s, the same rhythm.

A still photograph has none of this: no depth, no second view of a cube, no light changing. Shifting or warping the
photo, as v11 did, only moves the flat picture, which is why it looks fake. Real motion needs moving footage.

## How to get it (pick one)

1. **Generate it from our own five drink images (recommended).** Image-to-video keeps the exact look and colours
   of the plates and adds real liquid and ice motion. It runs here through the connected video service (Kling v3, pro,
   9:16, 5s, no sound). The quote is 25 credits a clip, about $0.25: 5 clips is about $1.25, and two takes each to
   choose from is about $2.50. The service's wallet is at 0 and needs a top-up first. The montage uses crops of the
   same clips, so no extra clips are needed.
2. **Shoot it.** Clear cup or glass, top-down macro, tripod locked, 4K at 50/60 fps, soft side light, 8–10s per
   drink: iced latte, matcha latte, cold brew, Spanish latte, iced karkadeh. No branded cups.
3. **Licensed stock (paid).** Free libraries (Mixkit, Coverr, Pexels, Pixabay) have no matching macro clips of these
   drinks. Paid libraries (Artgrid, Storyblocks, Envato) may, but the five would not match each other in look.

## Prompts for option 1 (one per plate, image-to-video)

Shared: *Extreme macro, top-down, locked-off camera, no camera movement. Slow, real liquid motion: the ice cubes drift
and turn slightly and independently, light and refraction shift through the ice, tiny bubbles rise and catch the
light. Calm, premium beverage commercial, shallow depth of field.*
Negative: *camera movement, zoom, pan, morphing, melting, warping shapes, new objects, hands, glass rim, straw, text,
logos, cuts*

## How it drops into the edit

- Each drink shot uses 1.55s from the middle of its clip, on a locked frame, hard cut on the note. Picture and
  sound timings stay as they are now.
- The cuts become motion-matched: each clip's 1.55s is chosen so its swirl carries into the next clip's swirl, so the
  eye follows the liquid across the cut.
- The montage uses 2-note crops of the same clips. The question's focus pull into the green and the first crop
  surfacing out of it stay as they are.
