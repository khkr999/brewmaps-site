# Feature story · The Map — `export/story-the-map.png` (1080×1920)

A premium, non-interactive story about one feature: the Map. "أكثر من 400 كوفي مختص على خريطة وحدة". The count comes
from the app's own Browse screen ("29 areas · 413 cafés"), written as "more than 400" so it stays true as cafés are
added. The map is the real app map crop (`appmap.png`, Dubai Marina, from `../coffee-routine-update/img/`), unaltered,
with no café names. No café photos, logos or emojis.

Design: deep forest ground with a faint street grid; a large Playfair "400" over Tajawal Arabic; the map as a card with a
thin cream edge; "افتح الخريطة، وشوف وش قريب منك" and the free-download line under it. All inside the story safe zone.

Re-render: `node render.js export/story-the-map.png story.html`.
