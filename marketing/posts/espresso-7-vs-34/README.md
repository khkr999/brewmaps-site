# AED 7 vs AED 34 — price Reel

Vertical video, 1080 × 1920, 10.5 seconds, silent. Seven typographic cards, hard cuts.

## The hook

Opens on "Here's what they don't tell you" and closes on "Screenshot this." Both are
withheld-fact hooks: they promise something specific and make you wait two beats for it.
That shape suits BrewMaps because the payoff is a number nobody else has.

## The cards

| # | Content | Motion |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Here's what they don't tell you about coffee in Dubai. | slow push in |
| 2 | Al Quoz — **7** — AED, one espresso | settles from oversize |
| 3 | JBR — **34** — AED, one espresso | settles from oversize |
| 4 | Same drink. Same city. | slow push in |
| 5 | We checked 78 cafés — **14** — AED, the median | slow push in, sand ground |
| 6 | Screenshot this. Before your next coffee run. | settles from oversize |
| 7 | @brewmaps — 812 cafés across the UAE. | slow push in |

Cards 2 and 3 are typographically identical, so the only thing that changes between them is
the number. That is what makes the jump land.

## The data

Every figure was parsed from the site's own area pages: 78 cafés list a plain espresso price,
the cheapest is AED 7 in Al Quoz, the dearest AED 34 in JBR, the median is AED 14. Both
extremes were checked against the source page before use.

**Re-pull before posting.** Prices move. Regenerate the cards if it has been more than a week.

`python3 cards.py && node render_v.js card-*.html && sh cmd.sh`
