# "I asked the sky" — cheap-prop Reel

Vertical video, 1080 × 1920, 9.6 seconds, silent. Built without anyone on camera.

## The bit

Text promises a sign from the heavens. The camera tilts up off a real café terrace into open
sky. What arrives is a cardboard cloud on a stick with an oddly specific instruction written
on it in marker. The stick is visible on purpose — the prop being obviously fake is the joke.

Nothing is explained and nobody appears. The handle lands in the last second.

## How this one was made

No footage was shot. The sky is the real JBR area photograph from `assets/derived/cafe-jbr.png`,
with its own sky sampled and extended upward to give the tilt somewhere to travel. The cloud is
drawn, because a drawn prop is what the bit calls for.

To shoot it for real instead: cut the cloud out of white card, tape it to a stick, hold it
against the sky and tilt up. It will look better than this, and the format is identical.

## Swapping the area

Edit the two handwritten lines in `cloud.html`, re-render it, then run `./build_video.sh`.
The counts live on the area pages — Al Quoz 29, Jumeirah 24, City Walk 12, Deira 10.
