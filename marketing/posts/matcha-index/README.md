## Caption

What does a matcha cost in the UAE?

We read the menus of 189 specialty cafés. The median listed price is AED 30, and half of them charge between AED 26 and 34.

Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah all land on AED 30. Khalifa City is the priciest area we measured at AED 35, Downtown the cheapest at AED 24. The area samples are small, so read them as a signal, not a rate.

Full index at brewmaps.app/matcha-index, link in bio.
