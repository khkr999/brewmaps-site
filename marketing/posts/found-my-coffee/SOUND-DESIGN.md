# على ذوقك · Ep. 1 "The Ladder": Sound design & audio cue sheet

16.6s · 9:16 · 24 fps · timecode is SS:FF (seconds:frames). Every sync point below is taken from the animation code, so the frame numbers are exact. The current export is silent. This mix gets baked into the final MP4; don't add music in Instagram.

## The idea

- **Sound idea:** The music tells the emotion and the SFX tell the comedy. 24% sounds low, muted, falling, minor and dry. 92% sounds high, bright, rising, major and sparkly. You should get "wrong for you / perfect for you" with your eyes closed.
- **Brand motif:** The 92% success chime and the end-card sonic logo are the same three rising notes (A–D–F#, key of D). The brand sound literally means "this one fits you". The 24% bonk is the opposite gesture: two muted notes falling a minor third (F→D).
- **Tone:** Playful, premium, witty, a little cinematic. Real-world textures (canvas, wood, glass, ice, water) with small musical accents on top. No cartoon trombones, no bouncy-castle boings, no slide-whistle clichés at full volume, no vocals.

Brand line on screen: **نفس الكوفي، مشروبين… واحد بس على ذوقك.** End card: **مو بس وين تروح… وش تشرب.**

## Mix & delivery notes

- Levels are relative to the splash peak (0 dB = loudest moment). Final mix: −14 LUFS integrated, −1 dBTP true peak, 48 kHz / 24-bit stereo, AAC 320 kbps in the MP4.
- Mono-compatible: check on a phone speaker. Pans stay within ±40% and nothing important relies on stereo.
- One shared short room reverb (~0.6s, small café) for the footsteps, pouch and ladder so they sit in the same space. Keep the bonk dry, the splash mostly dry with a short plate, and the chime and logo with a little shimmer.
- Music: one licensed track with stems (or a commissioned cue) so it stays one continuous piece. The deflate/glum sections are the same track with the melody and percussion stems muted, the bass held and a low-pass on. The bright section is the track's fuller second section. Library search terms: "quirky pizzicato", "playful caper", "whimsical modern strings", "light cinematic café". Instrumental only.
- If the track can't hit 2.05, 6.25 and 13.70 on downbeats at 100 BPM, cut it at those points and crossfade (2–3 frames) rather than nudging the picture.
- Instagram business accounts have limited in-app music, so bake the full mix into the exported MP4 and make sure the track licence covers branded social content.
