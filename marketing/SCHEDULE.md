## Tuesday 15 September — What a matcha costs in the UAE (carousel)

**Caption**

What does a matcha cost in the UAE?

We read the menus of 189 specialty cafés. The median listed price is AED 30, and half of them charge between AED 26 and 34.

Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah all land on AED 30. Khalifa City is the priciest area we measured at AED 35, Downtown the cheapest at AED 24. The area samples are small, so read them as a signal, not a rate.

Full index at brewmaps.app/matcha-index, link in bio.

Where do you get yours, and what do you pay?

#BrewMaps #Matcha #MatchaLatte #DubaiMatcha #UAEMatcha #DubaiCafes #AbuDhabiCafes #SpecialtyCoffee #UAECoffee

**Alt text:** Three slides: "AED 30. What a matcha costs in the UAE", median matcha price by emirate, and median matcha price by area.

**Source:** every figure is from the published BrewMaps Matcha Price Index (menus collected 4 August 2026). Nothing is rounded or estimated beyond what the index states.
