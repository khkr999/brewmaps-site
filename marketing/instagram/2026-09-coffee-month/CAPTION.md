# Your Coffee Month — BrewMaps Monthly, September 2026

Five slides, 1080 × 1350. A recurring monthly format: rebuild from a new Calendar screenshot each month.
Order is deliberate — EMOTION → EARNED → REDEEMED → DETAIL → CTA.

## ⚠ Before posting

**Both BrewPoints figures are placeholders.** Slide 2 shows 340 earned, slide 3 shows 200 redeemed
across three cafés. No BrewPoints or redemption screen was supplied, so neither number is verified
against the app. Replace them in `source/data.py` (`POINTS_PLACEHOLDER`, `REDEEMED_PLACEHOLDER`,
`REDEEMED_CAFES`) and re-render.

**Redemption claim.** Your terms page states BrewPoints are "in-app rewards with no monetary value".
Slide 3 says points were spent across three cafés. Check that wording matches how redemption actually
works in the app before this goes out — it is the one line on the carousel that makes a promise.

**Terminology check.** Slide 3 says CUPS POSTED, not CHECK-INS. The app's own calendar reads
"6 cups posted · 20 cafés · 5 days you didn't", and the August day view lists separate entries
marked "HAD COFFEE, DIDN'T POST" — so in your product a posted cup and a check-in are not the
same count. Slide 2 still says check-ins, because that is how BrewPoints are actually earned.
If 6 really is the check-in count, it is one word to change.

## The visual system

Forest green #1E3A14, night green #0E1F0A, warm sand #EAE1D1, cream #F4EFE6, pale green #9BC48A.
DM Sans throughout; Playfair Display italic only for the month name. Pale-green full stop and
question mark. Oversized numerals. Real café and check-in imagery only. One 76px margin.
Logo appears on two slides of four, never as a watermark.
