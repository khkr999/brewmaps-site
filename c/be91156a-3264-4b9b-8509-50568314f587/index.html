About
Caju Coffee House is a stylish, European-feeling café in Downtown Dubai known for beautifully plated brunch and breakfast dishes made with quality ingredients. It offers a cozy, welcoming atmosphere with views of the Burj Khalifa and attentive service, though order times can be slow during busy periods.
