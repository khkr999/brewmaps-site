About
JOE & THE JUICE is a vibrant juice and sandwich shop in Abu Dhabi offering quality specialty coffee alongside fresh juices and signature sandwiches. Known for balanced hot lattes, creative options like the Tunacado sandwich and Matcha Cloud drink, plus excellent service. Popular lunch destination with good quality offerings though prices can be steep during peak hours.
