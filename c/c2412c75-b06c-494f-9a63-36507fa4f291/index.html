About
Lila Molino + Cafe is an authentic Mexican restaurant in Dubai offering traditional dishes like Huevos Rancheros, fresh tacos, and churros with Arabic flavor influences. Known for freshly made tortillas and creative beverages like Tahini Honey latte, though service consistency and recipe execution have been noted as variable.
