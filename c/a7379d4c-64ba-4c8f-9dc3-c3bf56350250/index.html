About
Cross Coffee is a specialty coffee and bakehouse located at Al Amardi Mall in Dubai, known for exceptional espresso and filter coffee paired with delicious pastries and desserts. The café features a simple yet attention-grabbing interior with both indoor and outdoor seating, complemented by a welcoming and attentive staff team.
