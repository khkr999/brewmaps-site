About
The Besties Cafe is a beloved Dubai destination known for exceptional ice matcha lattes, premium cakes, and gelatos paired with fantastic Spanish coffee. With friendly staff who go above and beyond and a chilled atmosphere featuring both outdoor breezes and air-conditioned comfort, it offers a perfect blend of great food, welcoming service, and aesthetic appeal.
