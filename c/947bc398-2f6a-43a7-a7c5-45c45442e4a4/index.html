About
Adina's Coffee Shop specializes in traditional Romanian desserts and quality coffee in a warm, welcoming atmosphere. Reviews consistently praise their papanăși, nuci umplute, and cappuccino, with visitors noting an authentic Romanian ambiance. The café attracts repeat customers despite slower service and higher prices.
