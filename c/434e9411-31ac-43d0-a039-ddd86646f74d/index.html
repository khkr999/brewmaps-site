About
Dul Set Cafe is a BTS-themed café and tea house in Dubai offering exceptional Korean milk tea and an immersive K-pop experience. With welcoming staff and dedicated ambiance featuring member-inspired drinks and continuous BTS content on screens, it's a must-visit for fans and tea enthusiasts alike.
