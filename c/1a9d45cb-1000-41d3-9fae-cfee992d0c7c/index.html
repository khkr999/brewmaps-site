About
Paper Fig is a beloved bakery café in Muwaileh Commercial, Sharjah, known for exceptional baked goods and quality coffee. The restaurant combines a pleasant ambience with welcoming, professional staff and high-quality ingredients, though pricing is on the higher side for the location.
