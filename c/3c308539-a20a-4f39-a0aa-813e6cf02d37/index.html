About
RAW Coffee Company is a thoughtfully designed roastery in Al Quoz offering ethically sourced specialty coffees with expert preparation methods like V60 and espresso. The industrial-style space seamlessly combines a functional café, dedicated roastery, and retail section, complemented by excellent food including avocado toast and chicken burgers.
