About
Woodish is a newly opened restaurant and café in JVC known for consistently high-quality food across breakfast, brunch, and dinner. Reviews highlight exceptional dishes (pizza, risotto, mozzarella sticks, cheesecake), generous portions, and a notably warm, welcoming staff. The atmosphere is calm and cozy, with diners describing it as a gem that clearly prioritizes ingredient quality and customer experience.
