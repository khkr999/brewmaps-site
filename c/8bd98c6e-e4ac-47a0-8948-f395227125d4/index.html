About
Risen Café and Artisanal Bakery is a small, cozy specialty coffee spot in Jumeirah Village Circle known for high-quality, creative coffees and fresh baked goods like almond croissants. Staff members like Chamod and Manish are consistently praised for exceptional, personalized service. The café operates as part of the Risen chain and is located in a hotel lobby setting.
