About
Art House Cafe is a vibrant, colorful eatery located within the Etihad Modern Art Gallery in Abu Dhabi, offering specialty coffee, tea, and fresh breakfast bites alongside art experiences. The café combines a cozy, artistic atmosphere with quality food and excellent service, making it ideal for art lovers and those seeking a relaxing morning or afternoon.
