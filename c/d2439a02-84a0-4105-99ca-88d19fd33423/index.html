About
Mahaccino Cafe is a neighborhood gem in Abu Dhabi known for its signature Burgerccino and quality breakfast offerings. The cafe delivers great service and unique dishes like Turkish eggs, though some find the drink pricing (30 AED cappuccino) steep for the quality offered.
