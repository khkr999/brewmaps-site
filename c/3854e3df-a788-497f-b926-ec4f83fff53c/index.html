About
Project Chaiwala is a tea-led café concept on Alserkal Avenue in Dubai that successfully blends specialty chai culture with a cozy café atmosphere. Known for quality karak tea, specialty chai blends, and reasonable pricing, it offers an inviting space with cinema screening options and flexible seating.
