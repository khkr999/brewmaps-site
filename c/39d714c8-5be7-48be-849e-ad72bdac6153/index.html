About
Ethr ClubHaus is a beautifully designed specialty coffee café in Sharjah offering expertly crafted beverages like Brazilian V60 and cortado alongside traditional Emirati coffee. Known for exceptional pastries and baked goods including their signature aseeda dessert, labneh zaatar croissant, and French toast in a chill, cozy environment.
