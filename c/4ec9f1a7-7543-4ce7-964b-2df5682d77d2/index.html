About
The Coffee is a specialty coffee kiosk in Galleria Mall's P3 entrance serving Brazilian-roast beans prepared with Japanese precision. Known for exceptional espresso drinks like tiramisu lattes and iced vanilla, plus excellent pastries like banana bread. Staff, particularly Kim and Flavia, are praised for their professionalism and customer service.
