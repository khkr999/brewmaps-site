About
No. Fifty Seven Boutique Café is an upscale, feminine-focused café in Abu Dhabi near yacht parking, offering exceptional espresso and Spanish lattes in a cozy, aesthetically pleasing setting. Known for attentive service, healthy menu options, and signature Emirati Sheyati dish, it provides a relaxing retreat from the city's hustle.
