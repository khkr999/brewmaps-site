Delete your account and data

BrewMaps LLC · Last updated 13 September 2026

You can delete your BrewMaps account and the data attached to it at any time. You do not need to contact us first, and you do not need to give a reason.

Option 1 — In the app

  1. Open BrewMaps and go to the You tab.
  2. Open Settings (the gear icon).
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Option 2 — By email, without the app

Email hello@brewmaps.app from the address on your BrewMaps account, with the subject Account deletion request.

If you signed up with a phone number, include that number instead. We verify the request comes from the account holder, then delete the account and reply to confirm. We action requests within 30 days, normally much sooner.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your profile — username, display name, avatar, taste profile
  • Your email address and, where used, the phone number linked to sign-in
  • Your check-ins, drink ratings and notes
  • Your saved cafés, collections and lists
  • Photos and videos you posted, and your comments
  • Your direct messages and conversations
  • Who you follow and who follows you
  • Your BrewPoints balance, points history and reward redemptions

This is immediate and irreversible. Points and rewards cannot be restored afterwards.

What is kept

  • Anonymised usage records. We keep app-usage events — screens opened, searches run — with the account identifier permanently removed, so they can no longer be linked back to you. These are used only for aggregate statistics about how the app is used.
  • Records we are required to keep. Where UAE law obliges us to retain a transaction or tax record, we retain that record for the period the law requires and nothing more.

Cafés and other people's content

Café listings, menus and prices are business information about venues, not personal data about you, and are unaffected. If someone else's post mentions a café you visited, that post belongs to them and remains.

Questions

Email hello@brewmaps.app. See also our Privacy Policy.
