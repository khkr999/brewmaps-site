What stands out

Ajman records the lowest median at AED 25, about 17% below the AED 30 shared by Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah — though that figure rests on only 5 priced cafés. The more solid finding is that Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah land on the same AED 30 median, on much larger samples.
Khalifa City is the priciest area we measured (AED 35), while Downtown is the cheapest (AED 24) — a difference of AED 11 in median listed price. Both figures come from 4 and 4 cafés respectively — small samples, so read them as indicative.
Flavoured variants list higher. strawberry matcha at AED 32, coco matcha at AED 35, against AED 28 for a plain matcha latte — on 5 and 5 listings respectively, so read it as a signal rather than a rate.
Matcha is no longer a niche order. It appears on the menu of 189 cafés — and the single most common listing, matcha latte, appears 81 times.

Methodology

  • Source: published café menus in the BrewMaps catalogue, collected through image extraction, vision transcription and manual entry. Not delivery-platform prices.
  • Coverage: 189 cafés list matcha; 137 publish at least one readable price.
  • Which figure uses what: the headline median, the middle 50%, and every emirate and area comparison use one median price per café, across those 137 cafés. The variant table is the exception — it uses the 345 underlying priced listings, because a variant is a property of the listing, not the café.
  • Exclusions: 21 items across 5 cafés fell outside a plausibility band of AED 12–60 and were excluded pending verification. They are held back rather than corrected by guesswork.
  • One price per café, not per listing. A café's median matcha price is computed first, then emirate and area figures are the median of those café-level prices — so a café with a long menu doesn't outweigh one with a short menu.
  • Medians are used throughout rather than averages, so a single mispriced listing cannot move a figure.
  • Variant names are normalised before ranking: "Hot Matcha Latte" and "Matcha Latte (Hot)" count as Matcha Latte. Iced is kept separate, as it is priced differently.
  • The supplier file is limited to cafés with a drinks menu of 8 or more items, so a café with a stub menu isn't wrongly listed as "doesn't serve it".
  • Al Ain is included within Abu Dhabi, of which it is part.
  • Prices are a point-in-time snapshot from 4 August 2026 and change without notice. Sizes and grades vary between venues.

Snapshot

AED 750

Today's data, one-off. Non-exclusive.

  • 283 cafés with a real drinks menu, each flagged serves matcha: yes / no
  • 171 that list matcha, with the menu item as written
  • 127 of those publish a readable price — the rest list it without one
  • 112 cafés whose published menus don't list matcha, with a menu-fit score
  • Accounts can be supplied as verified briefs — observable menu evidence, prices, verified locations, public contacts, source links and verification dates.
  • Or as a flat CSV: café name, area, emirate, menu depth, matcha items and listed prices, menu-fit score.
  • Menu-fit score (0–5) counts how many adjacent premium categories a café already sells: Spanish latte, pistachio drinks, a manual brew method (V60, Chemex, Aeropress), cold brew, cortado. It is derived only from the café's own menu — no ratings, no review counts, no popularity signal, because those measure consumer traffic rather than whether a café would stock a category.
