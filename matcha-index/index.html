What stands out

Ajman is the cheapest emirate at a median of AED 25 — about 17% below the AED 30 that Dubai, Sharjah and Al Ain all share. The striking part is how flat the rest of the country is: pricing barely moves between emirates.
Khalifa City is the priciest area we measured (AED 35), while Downtown is the cheapest (AED 24) — a gap of AED 11 for the same drink.
Flavoured matcha carries a 20% premium. A plain matcha latte runs AED 28; strawberry and coconut versions sit near AED 32.
Matcha is no longer a niche order. It appears on the menu of 189 cafés — and the single most common listing, matcha latte, appears 70 times.

Methodology

  • Source: published café menus in the BrewMaps catalogue, collected through image extraction, vision transcription and manual entry. Not delivery-platform prices, which typically run 10–20% above dine-in.
  • Coverage: 189 cafés list matcha; 137 publish a price we could read. Percentiles use those 345 priced items.
  • Exclusions: 21 items across 5 cafés fell outside a plausibility band of AED 12–60 and were excluded pending verification. They are held back rather than corrected by guesswork.
  • Medians are used throughout rather than averages, so a single mispriced listing cannot move a figure.
  • Prices are a point-in-time snapshot from 4 August 2026 and change without notice. Sizes and grades vary between venues.
